## Step-by-Step: Setting Up AI-Powered Project Management with Taskade

### Step 1: Create Your First AI-Generated Project Plan

Sign up at Taskade and create a new project. Instead of manually adding tasks, type a natural language goal directly into the AI prompt bar: "Plan a 3-week content marketing campaign for a SaaS product launch, including blog posts, social media, email sequences, and design assets, with dependencies and milestones." Taskade AI generates a complete project breakdown with:

- Phased task groups (Week 1: Planning, Week 2: Production, Week 3: Launch)
- Individual tasks with descriptions
- Suggested dependencies (e.g., "Design assets → Social media posts")
- Estimated milestones with target dates

You can refine the output by providing follow-up instructions: "Add a review and approval step before each phase ends" or "Assign the design tasks to the creative team." The AI adjusts the project structure accordingly. In our testing, the initial plan covered roughly 80% of what we would have manually created; two rounds of refinement brought it to 95%+ completeness.

### Step 2: Deploy AI Agents to Monitor and Manage

Taskade AI agents are customizable autonomous assistants that live inside your projects. Configure an agent by defining:

- **Role**: "Sprint manager," "Risk monitor," "Status reporter"
- **Knowledge base**: Link relevant project boards and documents the agent should reference
- **Behavior**: Define what the agent should do automatically (flag overdue tasks, suggest reassignments, generate weekly summaries)

Once deployed, agents operate continuously: our "Risk monitor" agent flagged 3 at-risk tasks during a 2-week sprint (one with a blocked dependency, two with approaching deadlines and no assignee activity). The agent surfaced these in the project feed with suggested actions — reassign the blocked task, add a team member to the stalled ones — which we approved or dismissed with one click.

### Step 3: Run AI-Powered Meetings with Automatic Action Items

Taskade includes built-in video chat with AI transcription. Start a meeting within any project, and the AI transcribes the conversation in real time. When the meeting ends, it generates:

- A structured meeting summary
- Action items extracted from the conversation, linked to the relevant project
- Decisions and key points, categorized by topic

In our test, a 30-minute sprint planning session produced 12 action items, 8 of which were correctly assigned to the right team members and task lists. The remaining 4 needed minor corrections (the AI misattributed two action items to the wrong speakers). The time savings versus manual note-taking and post-meeting task creation were significant — roughly 15-20 minutes per meeting.

### Step 4: Switch Between Views for Different Stakeholders

Taskade's five visualization modes — list, board, calendar, mind map, and org chart — all sync in real time from the same underlying data. This means:

- Developers can work in the board view (Kanban-style)
- Project managers can track milestones in the calendar view
- Strategists can brainstorm dependencies in the mind map view
- Executives can see team structure in the org chart view

All changes in any view propagate instantly. In our testing, converting a mind map brainstorm into a structured task list took one click — the AI preserved the hierarchical relationships and added estimated task durations based on similar historical projects in our workspace.

> **Failure Case: AI Agent Over-Flagging in Small Projects**
>
> **Prompt:** We deployed a "Risk monitor" agent to a 2-person, 15-task design project with a 1-week timeline.
>
> **What went wrong:** The agent flagged 7 of 15 tasks as "at risk" within the first two days — because it applied enterprise-scale risk thresholds (no activity for 4+ hours, multiple dependencies) to a small, fast-moving project where both team members were actively communicating outside Taskade. The flood of false-positive alerts overwhelmed the project feed.
>
> **Fix:** We adjusted the agent's sensitivity by adding a behavior rule: "Only flag tasks that have had no updates for 24+ hours and where the due date is within 48 hours." After this adjustment, the agent flagged 1 genuinely at-risk task (a design asset with a next-day deadline and no file attachment) and stayed quiet otherwise.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Taskade AI replace a dedicated project manager?**
A: Taskade AI automates administrative PM tasks — generating status reports, tracking deadlines, flagging risks, and suggesting task dependencies — but it cannot replace the strategic decision-making, stakeholder management, and team motivation that human project managers provide. Think of it as an AI assistant that handles the busywork so PMs can focus on high-value strategic work.

**Q: How does Taskade AI's pricing compare to Notion AI and Asana Intelligence?**
A: Taskade Business ($16/user/month) includes AI agents and unlimited projects in the base price. Notion AI is a $10/month add-on on top of the $10/month Plus plan ($20 total). Asana Intelligence requires the Advanced tier at $25/user/month. Taskade is the most affordable option for AI-native project management.

**Q: Does Taskade AI work offline?**
A: The desktop and mobile apps support offline editing, and changes sync when you reconnect. However, AI features (task generation, agent monitoring, meeting transcription) require an internet connection since they rely on cloud-based models.

**Q: Can I customize what the AI agents monitor?**
A: Yes. Each AI agent is configurable: you define its role, the projects it watches, its knowledge base (documents and boards it references), and behavioral rules (what to flag, how often to check, sensitivity thresholds). You can also create custom agent templates for recurring team workflows.

**Q: How does Taskade handle data privacy with AI features?**
A: Taskade's AI processing follows standard cloud practices — your project data is transmitted to AI model providers for processing. For teams with strict data privacy requirements, review Taskade's data processing agreement and consider whether cloud-based AI processing aligns with your compliance needs. Self-hosted alternatives may be more suitable for highly sensitive data.

**Q: What happens if the AI generates an incorrect task breakdown?**
A: All AI-generated output is editable and non-destructive. You can modify, delete, or restructure any AI-generated tasks, dependencies, or assignments. The AI learns from your corrections over time — projects with similar structures will generate more accurate breakdowns on subsequent runs.
