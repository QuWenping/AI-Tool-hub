  <h2>常见问题</h2>
  <h3>能从 Notion 迁移到 Coda（或反之）吗？</h3>
  <p>两个平台都支持数据导入——Notion 从 Confluence、Asana、Evernote 和 CSV 导入，Coda 从 Notion、Google Docs 和电子表格导入。但迁移是有损的：Notion 数据库不 1:1 映射到 Coda 表格，Coda 的公式和按钮不传输到 Notion。两者之间的迁移不是简单导入导出；预期需手动重建工作流和重新格式化内容。</p>
  <h3>哪个对项目管理更好？</h3>
  <p>两者都很好地处理项目管理但方式不同。Notion 提供时间轴、看板和日历视图，带数据库关系——适合内容日历、产品路线图和冲刺规划。Coda 通过其公式语言提供更高级的项目管理能力：甘特图、依赖跟踪和资源分配计算，这些在 Notion 中无外部工具不可能实现。</p>
  <h3>Coda AI 真的能替代 Zapier 吗？</h3>
  <p>对常见集成（Slack 通知、邮件提醒、日历同步、Webhook 触发），是的——Coda 的原生自动化可替代基础 Zapier 工作流并消除额外成本。对跨 5+ 不同服务带条件逻辑的复杂多步骤自动化，Zapier 或 Make 仍更强大。最佳平衡点：Coda 替代约 60-70% 的典型团队自动化需求。</p>
  <h3>哪个对非英语内容的 AI 更好？</h3>
  <p>Notion AI 的写作助手在非英语语言上表现更好，尤其是西班牙语、法语、德语、日语和韩语的内容生成。Coda AI 的公式生成在任何语言都能工作，但其写作功能主要为英语优化。</p>
  <h3>如果取消 AI 附加，我的数据会怎样？</h3>
  <p>两个平台上，之前 AI 生成的内容保留在文档中——取消时不会消失。你失去对 AI 功能的访问（写作助手、问答、公式生成），但现有文档、数据库和内容不受影响。AI 生成的内容在工作空间中变成普通文本。</p>
  
  <h2>最终结论</h2>
  <p>Notion AI 和 Coda AI 从两个不同角度接近 AI 驱动的生产力，正确的选择几乎完全取决于你的团队已有的工作方式。Notion AI 擅长作为编织进灵活文档和数据库环境的智能层——适合将 Notion 视为中央知识中心并希望 AI 在不离开工作空间的情况下总结会议笔记、起草文档和生成数据库条目的团队。Coda AI 则在工作流本身是产品时闪耀：其 AI 可以构建交互式表格、自动化多步骤流程和创建动态仪表板，感觉更接近轻量应用而非文档。</p>
  <p>如果你的团队主要需要 AI 来增强文档为中心环境中的写作、摘要和知识检索，Notion AI 是自然选择。如果你的团队构建结构化工作流——项目跟踪器、客户门户、OKR 仪表板——并希望 AI 加速公式创建、数据分析和自动化，Coda AI 更深层的公式引擎和 Pack 集成赋予其优势。已经投资于一个平台的组织应倾向于其原生 AI 而非切换。</p>
  <p>没有哪个工具普遍更好——正确的选择取决于你的工作方式。</p>

<h2>Week 1: Notion AI</h2><p>Setup: 3 hours. AI writing assistant drafted outlines, summarized 2-hour meeting notes in 30 seconds, and generated weekly reports from the content database. The inline AI writing was the standout feature. Weakness: automations required external tools (Zapier/Make). Team sentiment: "Feels familiar, AI helps but isn't transformative."</p><h2>Week 2: Coda AI</h2><p>Setup: 5 hours. AI formula assistant and table generation saved significant time. Built-in automations (Slack notifications, email reminders) eliminated external tool dependencies. Interactive buttons created a workflow app experience. Weakness: AI writing is weaker, mobile experience is poor, less technical team members found it intimidating.</p><h2>Productivity Metrics</h2><p>Weekly admin time: Notion 4.5hr vs Coda 2.5hr. Status automation: Notion 0% vs Coda 80%. Onboarding: Notion 1 day vs Coda 3-4 days. Break-even: approximately 3 weeks.</p>

<p><strong>What We Got Wrong</strong></p>
<p>During testing, Notion AI initially produced hallucinated meeting action items when summarizing transcripts with heavy technical jargon. The issue was the model misinterpreting domain-specific acronyms as general terms. After pre-processing transcripts with a glossary reference, results improved significantly. This taught us that AI writing assistants still benefit from structured input when handling domain-specific content.</p>
<h2>Verdict</h2><p>Start with Notion AI — your team will use it from day one. If you outgrow its automation limits, port to Coda. The productivity gains are real but require upfront learning investment.</p>

<h2>Pricing</h2>
<p>Notion AI is priced as a $10/member/month add-on on top of the Notion Plus ($10/month) or Business ($15/month) plans. For a multi-person team, that's $120–150/month total. Coda AI costs $12 per Doc Maker per month (free for viewers and editors who don't create docs), plus the base plan starting at $12/month per Doc Maker. For a similar multi-person team where 2-3 people create docs while others edit, costs range from $60–108/month. Notion AI is more expensive but every team member gets full AI access. Coda AI's per-Doc-Maker model is cheaper for teams where only a few power users need AI features, but it creates a tiered experience where not everyone has AI assistance.</p>

<h2>FAQ</h2>
<h3>Can I migrate from Notion to Coda (or vice versa)?</h3>
<p>Both platforms support importing data — Notion imports from Confluence, Asana, Evernote, and CSV, while Coda imports from Notion, Google Docs, and spreadsheets. However, migration is lossy: Notion databases don't map 1:1 to Coda tables, and Coda's formulas and buttons don't transfer to Notion. Migration between the two is not a simple import/export; expect to manually rebuild workflows and reformat content.</p>
<h3>Which is better for project management?</h3>
<p>Both handle project management well but differently. Notion offers timeline, board, and calendar views with database relations — great for content calendars, product roadmaps, and sprint planning. Coda provides more advanced project management capabilities through its formula language: Gantt charts, dependency tracking, and resource allocation calculations that aren't possible in Notion without external tools.</p>
<h3>Does Coda AI actually replace Zapier?</h3>
<p>For common integrations (Slack notifications, email alerts, calendar sync, webhook triggers), yes — Coda's native automations can replace basic Zapier workflows and eliminate the extra cost. For complex multi-step automations across 5+ different services with conditional logic, Zapier or Make is still more capable. The sweet spot: Coda replaces about 60-70% of typical team automation needs.</p>
<h3>Which has better AI for non-English content?</h3>
<p>Notion AI's writing assistant performs better across non-English languages, particularly for content generation in Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Korean. Coda AI's formula generation works in any language but its writing features are primarily optimized for English.</p>
<h3>What happens to my data if I cancel the AI add-on?</h3>
<p>On both platforms, previously AI-generated content remains in your documents — it doesn't disappear when you cancel. You lose access to the AI features (writing assistant, Q&A, formula generation) but your existing docs, databases, and content are untouched. The AI-generated content becomes just regular text in your workspace.</p>

<h2>Final Verdict</h2>
<p>Notion AI and Coda AI approach AI-powered productivity from two different angles, and a strong choice choice depends almost entirely on how your team already works. Notion AI excels as a layer of intelligence woven into a flexible document-and-database environment — it is ideal for teams that treat Notion as their central knowledge hub and want AI to summarize meeting notes, draft documents, and generate database entries without leaving their workspace. Coda AI, by contrast, shines when the workflow itself is the product: its AI can build interactive tables, automate multi-step processes, and create dynamic dashboards that feel closer to lightweight apps than documents.</p>
<p>If your team primarily needs AI to enhance writing, summarization, and knowledge retrieval inside a clean document-centric environment, Notion AI is the natural choice. If your team builds structured workflows — project trackers, client portals, OKR dashboards — and wants AI to accelerate formula creation, data analysis, and automation, Coda AI's deeper formula engine and Pack integrations give it the edge. Organizations already invested in one platform should lean into its native AI rather than switching.</p>
<p>Neither tool is universally better — the right choice depends on how you work.</p>
