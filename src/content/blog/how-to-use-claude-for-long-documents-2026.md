## Why Claude for Long Documents?

Claude's 200K token context window can process approximately 150,000 words — roughly 500 pages — in a single session. This makes it the premier AI tool for working with full-length contracts, legal documents, research papers, and books.

## Key Use Cases

### Contract Review
Upload a full contract and ask Claude to: identify unusual clauses, flag potential risks, summarize key obligations, and compare against standard templates.

### Research Paper Analysis
Upload multiple papers and ask Claude to: synthesize findings, identify methodological differences, extract key statistics, and highlight gaps in the literature.

### Book Analysis
Upload an entire book and ask Claude to: provide chapter summaries, analyze character development, identify themes, and extract quotes on specific topics.

### Meeting Transcripts
Upload hours of meeting transcripts and ask Claude to: extract action items, identify decisions made, summarize by topic, and flag unresolved issues.

## Best Practices

1. **Start with a summary request** before diving into specific questions
2. **Ask targeted questions** about specific sections rather than "analyze this"
3. **Use follow-up questions** to drill deeper into areas of interest
4. **Request structured output** (tables, bullet points, JSON) for easier review
5. **Verify critical claims** — Claude can hallucinate details in very long documents

## Prompt Templates

**Contract review:** "Review this contract and identify: 1) Unusual or risky clauses, 2) Missing standard protections, 3) Key financial terms, 4) Termination conditions."

**Research synthesis:** "Compare these three papers on [topic]. Create a table showing: methodology, sample size, key findings, and limitations."
