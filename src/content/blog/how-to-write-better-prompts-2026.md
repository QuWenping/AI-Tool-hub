## The Prompt Formula

**Role + Context + Task + Format + Constraints**

### Example

**Weak:** "Write an email"

**Strong:** "You are a senior sales executive (Role). We are reaching out to a VP of Engineering at a mid-size SaaS company who attended our webinar (Context). Write a follow-up email that references the webinar topic, offers a personalized demo, and includes a clear call to action (Task). Keep it under 150 words and use a professional but warm tone (Format + Constraints)."

## Prompt Techniques

### Chain of Thought
Add "Think step by step" for complex reasoning tasks. This dramatically improves accuracy on math, logic, and multi-step problems.

### Few-Shot Prompting
Provide 2-3 examples of the desired output format. The AI will follow the pattern.

### Role Assignment
Assign a specific persona: "You are an expert [role] with 20 years of experience." This improves domain-specific responses.

### Iterative Refinement
Start broad, then refine. Use follow-up prompts like "Make it more concise" or "Add more technical detail."

## Common Mistakes

1. Being too vague ("make it better")
2. Asking multiple unrelated questions in one prompt
3. Not specifying the output format
4. Assuming the AI knows your context
5. Accepting the first response without refinement

## Prompt Templates

**Writing:** "Write a [type] about [topic] for [audience]. Tone: [tone]. Length: [length]. Include: [key points]."

**Analysis:** "Analyze this [document/data] and identify: 1) [pattern], 2) [anomaly], 3) [recommendation]. Present as a structured table."

**Creative:** "Generate [N] ideas for [purpose]. Each idea should include: name, one-line description, and why it works. Be unexpected but practical."
