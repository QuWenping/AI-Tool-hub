## Step 2: Text-to-Video Generation

**Basic workflow:**

1. Write a descriptive prompt in the text input field
2. Select aspect ratio (9:16 vertical recommended for social media)
3. Choose generation mode: Standard or Quality (longer generation time, potentially better results)
4. Click generate and wait approximately 60–120 seconds
5. Download the ~6-second video clip

**Prompt writing for Hailuo:**

Hailuo responds well to prompts that specify:
- **Subject** — "A young woman in a cream sweater..."
- **Action** — "...walking through a sunlit farmers market..."
- **Camera movement** — "...with a slow tracking shot..."
- **Lighting and atmosphere** — "...golden hour light filtering through market awnings"

During our testing, prompts describing clear physical actions (walking, turning, reaching, pouring) produced more convincing motion than prompts focused on static emotional states or abstract concepts.

**Practical example — social media product teaser:**

Prompt: "A sleek white smartphone rotating slowly on a dark surface, studio lighting with soft reflections on the screen, product photography style, subtle particle effects floating around the device"

This prompt reliably generated usable b-roll material for tech product promotions. The rotating motion was smooth, and the studio lighting interpretation was consistent across multiple generations.

## Step 3: Image-to-Video — The Strongest Mode

Image-to-video is where Hailuo performs best in our testing. By providing a starting frame, you anchor the model's output and reduce the variability that can plague text-to-video generation.

**Workflow:**

1. Upload a high-quality still image
2. Write a prompt describing the motion you want to see
3. Hailuo animates the image, adding natural movement while preserving the original subject and composition

**Effective image-to-video techniques:**

- **Product photos to short ads** — Upload a product shot and add motion: "camera slowly orbits the product, gentle lighting shift from left to right"
- **Portrait animation** — Upload a still portrait and add "subject smiles and turns head slightly toward camera, natural daylight"
- **Landscape to establishing shot** — Upload a landscape photo and add "slow pan across the scene, leaves rustling in gentle wind, clouds moving across sky"

In our tests, portrait animation produced the most natural results when the motion prompt was modest — a slight head turn, a subtle expression change, or gentle swaying. Aggressive motion prompts (running, jumping, dancing) introduced limb distortion and morphing artifacts.

**Failure Case: Complex Multi-Subject Scene**

We uploaded a group photo of four people at a dinner table and prompted: "Everyone laughing and raising glasses in a toast, warm candlelight." The output showed ghosting artifacts where faces blended into each other during the toast motion, and hand movements became distorted. Reducing the prompt to "subtle candlelight flicker, slight natural micro-movements of seated subjects" produced a usable clip. This suggests that Hailuo's current model works best with single-subject or simple two-subject scenes rather than complex multi-person interactions.

## Step 4: Building Longer Content Through Clip Stitching

Hailuo generates approximately 6-second clips per generation. For content longer than 6 seconds, you need a multi-clip workflow:

1. **Plan your shot sequence** — Outline 4–5 shots for a 24–30 second video
2. **Generate each clip separately** — Maintain consistent prompt language for visual continuity
3. **Assemble in a video editor** — Use CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve to stitch clips
4. **Add transitions** — Simple crossfades work best; complex transitions can emphasize frame-to-frame inconsistencies
5. **Add audio separately** — Hailuo does not generate audio; pair with ElevenLabs for voiceover, Suno/Udio for background music, or your own audio library
