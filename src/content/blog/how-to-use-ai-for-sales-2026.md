## Why AI Matters for Sales in 2026

Sales teams using AI are closing 30-50% more deals. AI eliminates busywork so reps can focus on building relationships.

## Key Applications

### AI-Powered Outreach
AI drafts personalized emails, LinkedIn messages, and follow-up sequences at scale. Best tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Jasper.

### Call Intelligence
AI transcribes and analyzes sales calls to extract objections, competitor mentions, next steps, and sentiment. Best tools: Fireflies, Otter.

### CRM Automation
AI automates data entry, lead scoring, and pipeline management. Best tools: Make, n8n, Zapier.

### Lead Research
AI researches prospects automatically — pulling company data, recent news, and social activity. Best tools: Perplexity, ChatGPT with browsing.

## Building Your AI Sales Stack

- **Week 1-2:** Start with call intelligence (Fireflies)
- **Week 3-4:** Add AI writing for outreach (ChatGPT or Claude)
- **Month 2:** Automate CRM workflows (Make or n8n)

## Real Results

A 12-person B2B SaaS team saw: 40% more outreach, 25% better response rates, 6 hours saved per rep per week, 15% higher close rates after 3 months.
