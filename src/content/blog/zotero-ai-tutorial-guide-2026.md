## FAQ

**Q: What can Zotero AI do?**

Auto-generate summaries of papers, extract key findings, suggest related papers in your library, assist with literature reviews, and help format citations. It understands your entire library as context for answering questions.

**Q: Is Zotero AI free?**

Zotero itself is free and open-source. The AI plugin offers a free tier with limited queries per month. The Premium plan at $10/month provides unlimited AI queries and advanced features like multi-paper synthesis.

**Q: How does Zotero AI compare to Elicit or Consensus?**

Zotero AI works within your existing reference library and PDFs. Elicit and Consensus search external databases. Zotero is better for managing and analyzing papers you already have; Elicit/Consensus are better for discovering new papers. Many researchers use both together.

**Q: Can Zotero AI write a literature review?**

Zotero AI can generate literature review drafts from selected references in your library, synthesizing themes and identifying research gaps. The output requires human review for accuracy and scholarly rigor, but it significantly accelerates the drafting process.

**Q: What reference formats does Zotero support?**

Zotero supports 10,000+ citation styles (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, etc.) and can automatically format bibliographies and in-text citations in word processors including Word, LibreOffice, and Google Docs.

**Q: Does Zotero AI work offline?**

The AI plugin requires internet connectivity for LLM queries. However, Zotero's core reference management (organizing, reading, annotating PDFs) works fully offline. You can sync your library for offline access and run AI queries when connected.

In our test with 80 computer science papers, this AI-assisted screening correctly identified 37 of 40 papers that met our inclusion criteria (92.5% sensitivity) while flagging only 3 papers incorrectly (7.5% false positive rate). This workflow is particularly valuable for graduate students and early-career researchers who may not yet have the domain expertise to rapidly screen papers in their field.

## Final Verdict

Zotero AI is the most practical AI research tool for academics who already use Zotero as their reference manager. The AI plugin integrates seamlessly into existing workflows, and the summarization and library-querying features directly address the most time-consuming aspects of literature management: screening papers, extracting key findings, and identifying thematic connections across a library.

At $10/month for Premium with unlimited queries, it is also the most affordable AI research tool in its class. The free tier's 50 queries per month is enough to evaluate whether the AI fits your workflow before committing.

The key limitation to understand is scope: Zotero AI works within your existing library. It does not search external databases for new papers. For discovery, pair Zotero AI with Elicit, Consensus, or Google Scholar — Zotero for managing and synthesizing what you have, and discovery tools for finding what you do not.

For graduate students managing dissertation libraries, research lab leads coordinating team readings, and academics who read 50+ papers per project, Zotero AI Premium is a strong value at $10/month. The time savings on summarization alone — roughly 10-15 minutes saved per paper — quickly justify the cost for anyone reading more than 10 papers per month.
