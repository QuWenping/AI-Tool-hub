## Core Tutorial: Getting More from Grammarly AI in 2026

### Step 1: Setting Up Grammarly Across All Platforms

Grammarly's strength lies in its ubiquity. Install it everywhere you write:

1. **Browser extension** (Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari): Visit grammarly.com and click "Add to Browser." This is the most important installation — it covers Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Twitter, and virtually every text field on the web
2. **Desktop app** (Windows/Mac): Download from grammarly.com/desktop. The app provides a distraction-free writing environment with full Grammarly capabilities and document management
3. **Mobile keyboard** (iOS/Android): Install the Grammarly Keyboard from the App Store or Google Play. Enable it in system keyboard settings for grammar checking in messaging apps, notes, and social media
4. **Microsoft Office add-in**: Download from the Microsoft AppSource store. This integrates Grammarly suggestions directly into Word and Outlook

The browser extension alone covers about 80% of most users' writing. Add the desktop app for long-form writing, and the mobile keyboard for on-the-go communication.

### Step 2: Understanding Grammarly's Suggestion Types

Grammarly organizes suggestions into color-coded categories that become intuitive with practice:

- **Red underlines** (Correctness): Spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors. These are the highest-confidence suggestions — accept most of them with minimal review
- **Blue underlines** (Clarity): Wordy sentences, passive voice, and readability improvements. Review these case by case — Grammarly sometimes suggests shorter sentences that change nuance
- **Purple underlines** (Delivery): Tone and formality adjustments. Especially useful for business communication where tone can be misinterpreted
- **Green underlines** (Engagement): Vocabulary variety and compelling language. Helpful for creative writing and marketing copy

**Key tip:** Open the sidebar (click the G icon) rather than relying on inline underlines alone. The sidebar shows an overall score, tone analysis, and a comprehensive list of all suggestions organized by category.

**Sample workflow for a business email:**
1. Draft the email freely without stopping to correct
2. Open the Grammarly sidebar and check the overall score
3. Review Red suggestions first (quick wins)
4. Check the tone detector — if it says "confident" but you want "friendly," adjust vocabulary
5. Read through Blue clarity suggestions, accepting those that improve flow
6. Final manual read-through for nuance that AI cannot catch

## FAQ

### Is Grammarly free?

The free tier covers basic spelling, grammar, and punctuation. Premium ($12/month) adds clarity rewrites, tone detection, full-sentence rewrites, and plagiarism checking. Business plans ($15/user/month) add style guides, analytics, and admin controls.

### Does Grammarly work with Google Docs?

Yes. The Chrome extension integrates directly with Google Docs. Suggestions appear as inline underlines and in the sidebar, similar to the Microsoft Word experience.

### Can Grammarly write content for me?

GrammarlyGO, the generative AI feature, can draft, rewrite, and brainstorm content based on prompts. It is designed as a writing assistant rather than a full content generator — you provide the direction and Grammarly helps execute. For long-form content generation from scratch, dedicated tools like Jasper or ChatGPT may be more suitable.

### Is Grammarly good for academic writing?

Grammarly Premium includes an academic mode that adjusts suggestions for formal writing conventions. The plagiarism checker scans against academic databases (ProQuest and others). Many universities have site licenses — check with your institution.

### Does Grammarly work in languages other than English?

Grammarly's grammar checking is primarily optimized for English. Some basic spell-check support exists for other languages, but the full feature set — tone detection, clarity rewrites, generative AI — is English-only.

### Will Grammarly make my writing sound robotic?

This is a common concern. Grammarly's suggestions are just that — suggestions. You can and should reject suggestions that flatten your voice. Using the tone detector to understand how your writing reads, rather than blindly accepting every suggestion, preserves your style while improving clarity. The goal setting feature (audience, formality, domain) helps Grammarly calibrate suggestions to your context.

### Is Grammarly secure for business use?

Grammarly Business includes SOC 2 compliance, SAML-based SSO, and data encryption. Text is processed on Grammarly's servers, which may not be acceptable for organizations with strict data residency requirements. Review Grammarly's trust page and your organization's data policies before deploying for sensitive content.

**Who should use Grammarly:** Non-native English speakers who want to improve written fluency, professionals who write high-stakes emails and reports daily, content teams managing multiple brand voices, and students who need grammar checking plus plagiarism detection.
