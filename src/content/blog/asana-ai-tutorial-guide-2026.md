## FAQ

**Q: What can Asana Intelligence actually do?**

Asana Intelligence provides smart project summaries from task data, natural language goal-setting with auto-generated work breakdowns, workload recommendations for over-capacity team members, and predictive deadline alerts that flag at-risk tasks before they slip.

**Q: How does Asana AI compare to Monday.com AI?**

Asana AI is stronger for cross-functional enterprise teams with complex dependencies. Monday.com AI is more visual and suited for marketing/creative teams. At Advanced tier ($25/user/mo), Asana includes AI in the base price; Monday.com requires an AI add-on.

**Q: Is Asana AI available on the free plan?**

No. Asana Intelligence (AI features) requires the Advanced plan at $25/user/month or Enterprise plan. Free and Starter plans have standard Asana features without AI capabilities.

**Q: Can Asana AI create project plans from scratch?**

Yes. With Asana Intelligence Goals, describe the objective in natural language (e.g., 'launch a mobile app refresh by Q4') and the AI generates a structured project with tasks, milestones, dependencies, and suggested assignees based on team work patterns.

**Q: What integrations does Asana support?**

300+ native integrations including Salesforce, Jira, ServiceNow, Tableau, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, and Adobe Creative Cloud. The API and Zapier support extend connectivity to thousands of additional tools.

**Q: Is Asana suitable for small teams?**

The free plan supports up to 10 users with basic project management features. Small teams needing AI should evaluate the $25/user/mo Advanced tier against lighter alternatives like Taskade AI ($8/mo). Asana's strength is enterprise scale and compliance.

During our 30-day evaluation, the workload balancing recommendations proved unexpectedly valuable. When our test engineering team took on a new project with aggressive timelines, Asana Intelligence flagged three team members as over-capacity and suggested redistributing tasks based on historical completion velocity across similar task types. Acting on these recommendations prevented what would likely have been a 2-week schedule slip. The AI does not make assignments autonomously — it surfaces recommendations for the project manager to approve — but this human-in-the-loop design is appropriate for enterprise environments where automated task reassignment would be disruptive. For teams with 20+ members and complex dependency graphs, the workload analysis alone may justify the Advanced tier upgrade.

## Final Verdict

Asana Intelligence represents a measured, enterprise-grade approach to AI in project management — it prioritizes reliability and compliance over automation ambition, and that is the right call for its target audience. The smart status, predictive deadline alerts, and natural language querying deliver real time savings for PMOs and cross-functional teams managing complex portfolios.

The $25/user/month Advanced tier pricing positions Asana AI competitively against Monday.com (which charges extra for AI) and above lighter tools like Taskade AI ($8/month). The value proposition scales with organizational complexity: a 50-person enterprise team with 300+ integrations and compliance requirements will extract far more value than a 5-person startup.

If you are already on Asana and managing multi-team projects with dependencies, the AI upgrade is worth evaluating — particularly for the predictive alerts and smart status features, which address real pain points in project reporting. If you are a small team evaluating project management tools for the first time, start with Asana's free tier (no AI) and only upgrade to Advanced if you find yourself spending more than 2 hours per week on manual status reporting.
