  <h2>第 1 步：选择你的 AI 音乐生成器</h2>
  
  <h3>从 Suno AI 开始（新手的游乐场）</h3>
  
  <p>Suno 是最好上手的 AI 音乐生成器，专为速度和易用性设计。其免费层级每天给你额度（约数首歌曲生成），网页界面在任何浏览器都能使用，无需技术设置。Suno 的 iOS 和 Android 移动应用让你随时随地创作音乐——在通勤途中哼唱一段旋律创意，到桌前时已变成一首完整歌曲。</p>
  
  <p><strong>为什么先选 Suno：</strong>生成速度（短歌曲不到一分钟）鼓励快速实验。你可以在几分钟内迭代一个歌曲创意的 10 个不同版本，保留每个最佳部分。相比早期 AI 音乐模型，Suno v4 产生更自然的人声措辞和情感变化。界面直观：输入提示词，选择风格标签，点击生成。没有旋钮、没有滑块、不需要音频工程知识。</p>
  
  <h3>进阶到 Udio（音乐人的升级）</h3>
  
  <p>当你在 Suno 上制作了几十首歌并开始注意到其局限——密集编曲上的混音浑浊、对歌曲结构的控制有限、无法编辑单个段落——是时候将 Udio 加入你的工具箱。Udio 由前 Google DeepMind 研究员创立，优先音频质量和创意控制而非速度。在我们的测试中，Udio 在乐器分离和精细编辑控制等方面通常表现更好。</p>
  
  <p><strong>何时切换：</strong>当你想要以下功能时迁移到 Udio：(a) 编辑歌曲的特定段落而不重新生成整首歌，(b) 提取单个乐器轨道（分离音轨）用于在 DAW 中混音，(c) 将哼唱或录制音频片段转化为完整制作的音轨，或 (d) 制作超过 4 分钟且带连贯多段结构的歌曲。许多音乐人保留两个订阅：Suno 用于快速创意构思，Udio 用于最终制作精修。</p>

  <h2>第 2 步：掌握音乐提示词艺术</h2>
  
  <h3>好音乐提示词的构造</h3>
  
  <p>AI 音乐生成器对提示词的响应方式不同于 AI 图像或文本生成器。好音乐提示词有四个组成部分：</p>
  
  <ol>
  <li><strong>流派和子流派：</strong>要具体。与其说"摇滚"，不如尝试"带鞋钉吉他和梦幻流行人声和声的独立摇滚"。与其说"电子"，不如尝试"带深沉贝斯、氛围铺垫和驱动性四拍节拍的旋律浩室"。Suno 和 Udio 都处理流派组合良好——"合成波加爵士萨克斯独奏"或"管弦民谣带氛围质感"是产生有趣结果的有效提示词。</li>
  <li><strong>情绪和情感：</strong>描述感受：忧郁、欣快、紧张、怀旧、胜利、苦乐参半、俏皮。情感描述词对和弦进行和人声表达有很强影响。对比"一首悲伤的歌"和"一首关于告别童年家园的苦乐民谣"——后者给 AI 一个叙事锚点，塑形整个创作。</li>
  <li><strong>乐器：</strong>如果你心中有特定声音，命名具体乐器：指弹木吉他、Rhodes 电钢琴、808 贝斯、弦乐四重奏、失真强力和弦、钢片琴、萨克斯。你的乐器越具体，输出就越接近你的心中参考。</li>
  <li><strong>结构提示：</strong>Suno 和 Udio 都受益于结构指导："主歌-副歌-主歌-副歌-桥段-副歌"或"氛围铺垫渐入带来爆发性跌落"或"开始极简只有钢琴和人声渐次加层"。在 Suno 中，你可以在自定义模式中使用特定歌词加结构标签如 [主歌]、[副歌]、[桥段]、[尾声]，这给你对歌曲编排的精细控制。</li>
  </ol>
  
  <h3>示例提示词（新手到高级）</h3>
  
  <ul>
  <li><strong>新手：</strong>"一首关于和朋友夏日冒险的欢快流行歌曲"</li>
  <li><strong>中级：</strong>"梦幻流行带模糊吉他和呼吸感女声，怀旧朦胧，像在回忆一个从未发生的夏天，主歌-副歌-主歌-副歌-桥段-副歌"</li>
  <li><strong>高级（Suno 自定义模式）：</strong>[主歌] "霓虹灯光映在湿沥青上 / 城市在凌晨三点哼唱摇篮曲" [副歌] "我们带电，我们活着 / 在半明半暗中追逐影子"</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h2>第 3 步：从 AI 生成到成品曲目</h2>
  
  <h3>阶段 1：创意构思（Suno）</h3>
  
  <p>在 Suno 上用稍有不同的提示词生成你歌曲创意的多个变体。不要执着于任何单次生成——将此阶段视为头脑风暴。保存 3-5 个候选最佳版本，捕获你想要的氛围。聆听：强烈的旋律钩子、自然的人声表达、有趣的和弦变化和正确的情感基调。Suno 的扩展功能让你继续向前扩展生成，这在前 30 秒高质量但其余部分需要工作时很有用。</p>
  
  <h3>阶段 2：结构和精修（Udio）</h3>
  
  <p>把你最喜欢的 Suno 生成为参考，在 Udio 上重现概念以获得更高的制作质量。使用 Udio 的扩展生成能力（可用性取决于当前方案和音轨类型）创建完整的歌曲结构。如果特定段落需要修正——一个弱桥段、一个不达标的旋律、一个感觉笨拙的过渡——使用 Udio 的修补工具。选择有问题的段落并只重新生成那些不到一分钟的部分，同时保持音轨其余部分不变。此编辑工作流目前 Suno 不支持（仅支持向前扩展）。</p>
  
  <h3>阶段 3：分离音轨和 DAW 集成（Udio Pro）</h3>
  
  <p>如果你计划在流媒体平台发布歌曲或在视频项目中使用，使用分离音轨工具（Pro 方案功能）从 Udio 音轨提取分离轨。这给你单独的人声、鼓、贝斯和其他乐器的 WAV 文件。将这些导入 DAW 如 GarageBand（免费、新手友好）、Ableton Live 或 Logic Pro 以：</p>
  
  <ul>
  <li>调整乐器间音量平衡</li>
  <li>为单个音轨添加混响、延迟、EQ 或压缩</li>
  <li>用真实贝斯录音或 MIDI 乐器替换 AI 贝斯线</li>
  <li>在 AI 器乐上叠加你的人声或反之</li>
  <li>添加淡入、淡出和母带效果达到专业完成度</li>
  </ul>
  
  <p>Udio 的音频到音频功能在此特别强大：用手机哼唱一段副旋律，上传，并将其转化为与现有音轨融合的完整制作乐器层。这以一种感觉协作而非全自动的方式弥合了 AI 生成和人类音乐创意之间的鸿沟。</p>
  
  <h2>第 4 步：发布和分享你的音乐</h2>
  
  <h3>分发选项</h3>
  
  <ul>
  <li><strong>流媒体平台（Spotify、Apple Music、Amazon Music）：</strong>付费方案通常授予商业使用权，但用户应在任何商业发布前核实最新许可条款。使用 DistroKid、TuneCore 或 CD Baby 等分发商上传曲目。注意：纯 AI 生成歌曲可能面临版权注册挑战——添加大量人类创意输入（DAW 混音、现场乐器层、原创歌词）增强你的权利主张。</li>
  <li><strong>YouTube 和社交媒体：</strong>导出为 MP3 或 WAV 并与视频内容同步。两个平台的付费层级允许变现。对 YouTube，考虑使用 Canva 或 CapCut 创建简单的可视化视频来配合你的曲目——静态专辑封面视频在算法上表现弱于动画可视化。</li>
  <li><strong>SoundCloud 和 Bandcamp：</strong>最适合建立初始受众和获取反馈。SoundCloud 社区对 AI 辅助音乐持接纳态度，只要你透明你的过程。Bandcamp 让你设置自己的价格并建立支持者的邮件列表。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h3>许可和署名</h3>
  
  <p>免费方案通常包含使用限制。商业权利取决于平台、订阅层级和当前许可协议。在任何商业发布前始终在每个平台官网核实最新条款。始终检查当前条款因为 AI 音乐许可是一个不断演变的法律领域。如果你与其他艺术家合作、为客户制作音乐或提交到同步许可库，查阅平台的商业条款文件并在高风险项目考虑法律建议。</p>
  
  <h2>常见新手错误及如何避免</h2>
  
  <ul>
  <li><strong>模糊提示词：</strong>"一首好歌"没有给 AI 任何可工作的素材。花 60 秒构建一个带流派、情绪和乐器的具体提示词。输出质量的差异是巨大的。</li>
  <li><strong>满足于第一次生成：</strong>AI 音乐是数量游戏。在判断概念是否奏效前生成 10+ 个版本的任何创意。最佳输出通常来自第 5-10 次生成，当 AI 探索了更多创意空间。</li>
  <li><strong>忽略歌曲结构：</strong>如果你不引导，AI 可能产生散乱无结构的音轨。在 Suno 中使用自定义模式加 [主歌][副歌] 标签，或在 Udio 提示词中包含结构提示。清晰的结构是区分歌曲和声景的关键。</li>
  <li><strong>跳过 DAW 步骤：</strong>即使是领先的 AI 生成曲目也会从 DAW 中少量基本混音中受益。调整音量、添加母带链和清理频率冲突将曲目从"AI 演示"提升到"可发布歌曲"。</li>
  <li><strong>不备份你的最爱：</strong>AI 平台快速演变，如果模型更新或条款变化，生成可能变得不可访问。下载你可能想保留的每一首曲目，同时作为 MP3（分享用）和 WAV（未来编辑用）。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h2>前路：你的前 30 天</h2>

<h2>Step 2: Master the Art of Music Prompting</h2>

<h3>The Anatomy of a Good Music Prompt</h3>

<p>AI music generators respond to prompts differently than AI image or text generators. A good music prompt has four components:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Genre and subgenre:</strong> Be specific. Instead of "rock," try "indie rock with shoegaze guitars and dream-pop vocal harmonies." Instead of "electronic," try "melodic house with deep bass, atmospheric pads, and a driving four-on-the-floor beat." Both Suno and Udio handle genre combinations well—"synthwave with jazz saxophone solo" or "orchestral folk with ambient textures" are valid prompts that produce interesting results.</li>
<li><strong>Mood and emotion:</strong> Describe the feeling: melancholic, euphoric, tense, nostalgic, triumphant, bittersweet, playful. Emotional descriptors have a strong impact on chord progressions and vocal delivery. Compare "a sad song" to "a bittersweet ballad about saying goodbye to your childhood home"—the latter gives the AI a narrative anchor that shapes the entire composition.</li>
<li><strong>Instrumentation:</strong> Name specific instruments if you have a sound in mind: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, Rhodes electric piano, 808 bass, string quartet, distorted power chords, glockenspiel, saxophone. The more specific your instrumentation, the closer the output will match your mental reference.</li>
<li><strong>Structure hints:</strong> Both Suno and Udio benefit from structural guidance: "verse-chorus-verse-chorus-bridge-chorus" or "intro with ambient pads building into an explosive drop" or "starts minimal with just piano and voice, gradually adds layers." In Suno, you can use the Custom mode to write specific lyrics with structural tags like [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro], which gives you granular control over the song's arrangement.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Example Prompts (Beginner to Advanced)</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Beginner:</strong> "An upbeat pop song about summer adventures with friends"</li>
<li><strong>Intermediate:</strong> "Dream-pop with washed-out guitars and breathy female vocals, nostalgic and hazy, like remembering a summer that never happened, verse-chorus-verse-chorus-bridge-chorus"</li>

<h2>Step 3: From AI Generation to Finished Track</h2>

<h3>Phase 1: Ideation (Suno)</h3>

<p>Generate multiple variations of your song idea on Suno using slightly different prompts. Don't get attached to any single generation—treat this phase as brainstorming. Save a leading 3-5 versions that capture the vibe you're going for. Listen for: a strong melodic hook, natural vocal delivery, interesting chord changes, and the right emotional tone. Suno's Extend feature lets you continue a generation forward, which is useful if the first 30 seconds are high-quality but the rest needs work.</p>

<h3>Phase 2: Structure and Polish (Udio)</h3>

<p>Take your favorite Suno generation as a reference and recreate the concept on Udio for higher production quality. Use Udio's extended generation capabilities (availability depends on current plan and track type) to create a full song structure. If specific sections need fixing—a weak bridge, a vocal line that didn't land, a transition that feels clunky—use Udio's Inpainting tool. Select the problematic section and regenerate just those under a minute while keeping the rest of the track intact. This editing workflow is not currently supported on Suno (which only supports extending forward).</p>

<h3>Phase 3: Stems and DAW Integration (Udio Pro)</h3>

<p>If you plan to release the song on streaming platforms or use it in a video project, extract the stems from your Udio track using the Stem Splitter (Pro plan feature). This gives you separate WAV files for vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments. Import these into a DAW like GarageBand (free, beginner-friendly), Ableton Live, or Logic Pro to:</p>

<ul>
<li>Adjust volume levels between instruments</li>
<li>Add reverb, delay, EQ, or compression to individual tracks</li>
<li>Replace the AI bass line with a live bass recording or MIDI instrument</li>
<li>Layer your own vocals over the AI instrumental or vice versa</li>
<li>Add fade-ins, fade-outs, and mastering effects for a professional finish</li>
</ul>

<p>Udio's audio-to-audio feature is particularly powerful here: hum a counter-melody into your phone, upload it, and transform it into a fully produced instrumental layer that blends with your existing track. This bridges the gap between AI generation and human musical creativity in a way that feels collaborative rather than fully automated.</p>

<h2>Common Beginner Mistakes and How to Avoid Them</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Vague prompts:</strong> "A good song" gives the AI nothing to work with. Spend 60 seconds crafting a specific prompt with genre, mood, and instrumentation. The difference in output quality is dramatic.</li>
<li><strong>Settling for the first generation:</strong> AI music is a numbers game. Generate 10+ versions of any idea before judging whether the concept works. The best outputs often come from generations 5-10 when the AI has explored more of the creative space.</li>
<li><strong>Ignoring song structure:</strong> AI can produce meandering, structureless tracks if you don't guide it. Use Suno's Custom mode with [Verse][Chorus] tags, or include structure hints in your Udio prompt. A clear structure is what separates a song from a soundscape.</li>
<li><strong>Skipping the DAW step:</strong> Even a leading AI-generated track benefits from a small amount of basic mixing in a DAW. Adjusting levels, adding a mastering chain, and cleaning up any frequency clashes elevates a track from "AI demo" to "releasable song."</li>
<li><strong>Not backing up your favorites:</strong> AI platforms evolve rapidly, and generations can become inaccessible if models are updated or terms change. Download every track you might want to keep as both MP3 (for sharing) and WAV (for future editing).</li>
</ul>
