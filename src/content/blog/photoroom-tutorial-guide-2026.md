## Core Tutorial: Creating Product Photos with PhotoRoom

### Step 1: Getting Started

PhotoRoom is available as a mobile app (iOS and Android) and a web application. The mobile app is the more mature platform and provides the most complete feature set. Download from your app store or visit the PhotoRoom website for the web version.

The free tier includes background removal and basic templates with a small watermark. The Pro subscription removes the watermark and unlocks batch processing, premium templates, and advanced AI scene generation.

**Screenshot description:** *PhotoRoom app home screen on an iPhone showing a grid of recently edited product photos. A prominent "Create New" button sits at the bottom center. A banner at the top highlights the Pro subscription with "Try Pro Free" CTA.*

### Step 2: Removing the Background

The core workflow starts with background removal:

1. Tap "Create New" and select a photo from your camera roll or take a new photo
2. PhotoRoom automatically detects the subject and removes the background — this takes approximately 2–3 seconds on a recent iPhone
3. The result shows your product on a transparent or white background
4. Use the "Erase" and "Restore" brushes to manually refine the edges if needed

**Practical tip:** For clean background removal, photograph products against a contrasting background with even lighting. A product on a white table against a white wall is harder for the AI to separate than the same product on a dark surface against a light wall.

**Screenshot description:** *PhotoRoom editing screen showing a pair of sneakers after background removal. The shoes appear on a checkered transparency grid. A toolbar at the bottom shows Erase, Restore, and Edge Refine tools. The original photo (with cluttered room background) appears as a thumbnail for comparison.*

### Step 3: Adding an AI-Generated Background

PhotoRoom's scene generation goes beyond simple background removal:

1. Tap "Background" and select from templates (studio, lifestyle, seasonal) or choose "AI Background"
2. For AI Background, type a scene description — for example, "marble surface with soft natural light and a potted plant in the background"
3. PhotoRoom generates the scene and places your product in it, adjusting lighting and shadows to match
4. Browse additional generated variations with a swipe

**Example prompts we tested:**

- "White marble countertop with morning sunlight streaming from a window, minimal, clean" — produced a bright, professional studio look suitable for jewelry and cosmetics
- "Rustic wooden table with warm ambient lighting, coffee shop atmosphere" — produced lifestyle imagery for artisanal food products and handmade goods
- "Minimal gradient background, soft pastel, product photography style" — produced clean marketplace-style images suitable for Amazon or Etsy listings

## Failure Case: The Transparent Perfume Bottle

**The Product:**

A clear glass perfume bottle with gold accents and a reflective metallic cap, photographed against a white wall with indirect window light.

**What Went Wrong:**

PhotoRoom's background removal struggled with the transparent glass body. The AI treated the see-through areas as "background" and removed them, leaving a fragmented, partial bottle:

- The glass body was partially transparent, showing the AI-generated background through the bottle — but the edges were jagged where the AI could not determine the glass boundary
- The metallic cap's reflections were interpreted as separate objects and partially cropped
- The gold accents, which were thin and reflective, were partially erased along their edges
- The bottle's shadow on the original white surface, which provided depth cues, was completely removed — leaving the bottle looking flat and floating

**How We Fixed It:**

We re-shot the perfume bottle with three adjustments:

1. **Darker background:** Placed the bottle against a dark gray fabric — the contrast between clear glass and a dark surface helped the AI identify the glass body boundary
2. **Backlighting:** Added a small LED light behind the bottle to create a rim light effect, which defined the glass edges more clearly
3. **Manual edge refinement:** After AI background removal, used the Restore brush to manually paint back areas of the glass body that the AI had incorrectly removed

The re-shot and manually refined result was usable, though it required approximately 10 minutes of manual touch-up. Key lessons: transparent, reflective, and highly detailed products (jewelry, glassware, metallic objects) benefit from careful photography setup — contrasting backgrounds and edge definition lighting — rather than relying entirely on post-processing AI correction.

## FAQ

### 1. Does PhotoRoom work for all product types?

PhotoRoom handles most product categories well, particularly clothing, home goods, packaged food, cosmetics, and opaque electronics. The categories that require more manual refinement are transparent objects (glass bottles, windows), highly reflective surfaces (mirrors, polished metal), and fine-detail items (jewelry chains, hair accessories). For these products, expect to spend 2–5 minutes per image on manual edge refinement.

### 2. Can I use PhotoRoom images directly on Amazon?

Yes, with a Pro subscription for watermark-free exports. Amazon requires product images without watermarks, logos, or text overlays on the main image. PhotoRoom's Amazon export preset (2000×2000px, white background, JPEG) meets these technical requirements. The AI-generated backgrounds are suitable for secondary listing images, but Amazon's main image policy requires a pure white background — use PhotoRoom's plain white background option for the primary listing image.

### 3. How is PhotoRoom different from Remove.bg?

Remove.bg does one thing: removes backgrounds. It does this well and quickly, but once the background is removed, you need another tool for the next step. PhotoRoom removes the background and provides tools for the entire workflow: adding new backgrounds (AI-generated or templates), batch processing, retouching, and exporting in marketplace-ready formats. If you only need background removal, Remove.bg is simpler. If you need a complete product photography workflow, PhotoRoom covers more of the pipeline.

### 4. Does the AI background generation look realistic?

In most cases, yes — PhotoRoom does a strong job matching lighting direction, color temperature, and shadow placement between the product and the generated background. Products appear grounded in the scene rather than floating. Less natural results occur when the original product photo has strong directional lighting that conflicts with the generated scene's implied light source, or when the product has complex, irregular edges that the AI cannot cleanly blend.

### 5. Is the mobile app better than the web version?

Currently, the mobile app (iOS and Android) is the more mature and feature-complete platform. The web version is functional but has fewer templates, a simpler scene generation engine, and no batch processing. For serious use, the mobile app is recommended. PhotoRoom has stated that web feature parity is a development priority.

### 6. Can I process videos with PhotoRoom?

PhotoRoom's current focus is still photography. Background removal for video is not supported. For video background removal, tools like Runway or CapCut offer AI-powered alternatives.

### 7. What happens to my photos — are they stored or used for training?

PhotoRoom's privacy policy states that user photos are processed for the purpose of providing the service and are not used to train AI models without consent. Pro subscribers can opt out of data usage for model improvement. Photos are stored on PhotoRoom's servers while you maintain an account and are deleted upon account closure.
