  <h2>定价：不同价值主张</h2>
  
  <p>Leonardo AI 的定价纯粹用于 AI 图像生成。$12/月 Apprentice 方案获得 8,500 快速生成代币——足够严肃的创意工作。Canva Pro $15/月获得 AI 图像生成加上整个 Canva 设计生态：模板、库存照片、视频编辑、品牌工具包、排期和团队协作。如果你只需要 AI 图像，Leonardo 提供更好价值。如果你需要 AI 图像加上将其变成完整设计所需的一切，Canva Pro 非常划算。</p>

  <h2>图像质量与控制</h2>
  
  <h3>Leonardo AI：为需要控制的创作者打造</h3>
  
  <p>Leonardo 的核心优势是控制。在自己的角色设计或艺术风格上微调模型，然后生成保持一致性的无限变化。平台提供：</p>
  
  <ul>
  <li><strong>自定义模型训练：</strong>上传 10-30 张图像并训练一个以你的确切风格生成的模型。游戏工作室用此保持数千资产变化的视觉一致性。</li>
  <li><strong>ControlNet 集成：</strong>姿势转图像、边缘转图像和深度转图像给你精确的构图控制。草绘一个粗略姿势，Leonardo 以你选择的风格渲染。</li>
  <li><strong>提示词辅助：</strong>Leonardo 的提示词生成工具帮你编写有效提示词，降低学习曲线而不牺牲能力。</li>
  <li><strong>社区模型：</strong>访问数千个社区训练的模型用于特定风格——像素艺术、油画、动漫、照片级写实等。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h3>Canva AI：为市场速度打造</h3>
  
  <p>Canva AI 的优势不是原始图像生成质量，而是无缝集成到市场工作流。生成产品照片，放入 Instagram 模板，添加品牌颜色和 Logo，用 Magic Write 写文案，排期发布——全在 Canva 内完成。单项 AI 功能可能不是最强的，但组合工作流领先：</p>
  
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Magic Media：</strong>直接在 Canva 设计中生成图像。质量不错——相当于中级 Stable Diffusion 模型——但你很少离开设计画布。</li>
  <li><strong>魔法橡皮擦和魔法扩展：</strong>在 Canva 编辑器内清理 AI 生成图像或扩展背景。</li>
  <li><strong>Magic Write：</strong>AI 文案生成，为你生成的图像配标题、标题和广告文案。</li>
  <li><strong>批量创建：</strong>为 A/B 测试大规模生成不同文字或图像的设计变体。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h2>优缺点</h2>
  
  <h3>Leonardo AI</h3>
  <p><strong>优点：</strong>自定义模型微调实现风格一致；高级 ControlNet 引导实现精确构图；慷慨的免费层级（150 张/天）；数千训练模型的强大社区；专精于游戏素材和概念艺术。</p>
  <p><strong>缺点：</strong>无设计或布局工具——图像必须导出到其他应用做最终处理；微调和 ControlNet 功能有学习曲线；对普通用户界面可能感觉技术化；无品牌工具包或模板系统。</p>
  
  <h3>Canva AI</h3>
  <p><strong>优点：</strong>完整设计到发布工作流在一个平台；250,000+ 模板即时产出专业外观；实时团队协作；品牌工具包维护视觉一致性；Magic Write 为图像配 AI 文案。</p>
  <p><strong>缺点：</strong>AI 图像质量落后于专用工具；仅 50 次终身免费 AI 生成；无模型微调或 ControlNet；与专用生成器相比风格多样性有限；目前不匹配 Leonardo 或 Midjourney 的创意深度。</p>
  
  <h2>按用例选择胜者</h2>
  
  <h3>何时选择 Leonardo AI</h3>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>游戏开发：</strong>通过自定义模型生成风格一致的角色设计、物品图标、环境概念和 UI 元素。</li>
  <li><strong>概念艺术和插画：</strong>在你的艺术风格上微调模型，为客户展示或作品集生成变体。</li>
  <li><strong>角色设计：</strong>使用 ControlNet 创建姿势、服装和表情一致的完整角色设定图。</li>
  <li><strong>AI 艺术探索：</strong>如果你是喜欢尝试不同模型、参数和工作流的 AI 艺术爱好者，Leonardo 提供更多深度。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h3>何时选择 Canva AI</h3>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>社交媒体内容：</strong>生成图像并直接放入 Instagram、TikTok 或 LinkedIn 模板，带品牌标识。</li>
  <li><strong>市场物料：</strong>创建传单、演示文稿、广告和邮件头图，AI 生成视觉加文案——全在一个工具内。</li>
  <li><strong>小企业主：</strong>如果你需要制作市场材料而无需雇设计师，Canva 的 AI + 模板组合是最完整的解决方案。</li>
  <li><strong>团队协作：</strong>Canva 的实时协作、评论和品牌工具包共享使其成为市场团队的明确选择。</li>
  </ul>
  
  <h2>谁该选哪个</h2>
  <p>两者服务于不同需求。快速决策指南：</p>
  
  <h2>常见问题</h2>
  
  <h3>能同时使用 Leonardo AI 和 Canva AI 吗？</h3>
  <p>当然——许多专业人士都这样做。在 Leonardo 中生成高质量图像，导出后导入 Canva 进行布局、文字叠加和品牌处理。这给你 Leonardo 更灵活的图像生成加上 Canva 更灵活的设计工具。少量工作流摩擦对重要项目来说值得质量提升。</p>
  
  <h3>哪个图像质量更好？</h3>
  <p>Leonardo AI 产生明显更好的图像，尤其使用自定义微调模型时。Canva 的 AI 图像对社交媒体和基础营销足够但缺乏专用 AI 图像工具的细节、风格多样性和控制。如果图像质量是首要考虑，选择 Leonardo 或 Midjourney 而非 Canva AI。</p>
  
  <h3>Canva AI 包含在 Canva Pro 中吗？</h3>
  <p>Canva Pro 包含 Magic Media（AI 图像生成）每月有一定额度。确切数量有所不同但比免费层级的 50 次终身生成慷慨得多。Canva for Teams 和企业方案有更高限制。无法单独购买额外 AI 积分——它们与设计订阅捆绑。</p>
  
  <h3>Leonardo AI 能生成商业用途图像吗？</h3>
  <p>能，Leonardo AI 授予平台上生成图像的商业使用权，包括使用自定义微调模型生成的。然而，AI 生成图像和版权的法律环境仍在演变。如果你计划将生成图像用于商品或大规模商业产品，请咨询所在法域的 AI 版权法律指导。</p>
  
  <h3>Canva AI 支持团队工作流吗？</h3>
  <p>是的，Canva 的团队功能是主要差异化因素。多名团队成员可实时协作设计、留下评论、共享含批准颜色和字体的品牌工具包，并管理权限。Leonardo AI 有基础团队共享，但远不及 Canva 的协作深度。对市场团队，仅此往往决定选择。</p>
  
  <p><strong>我们踩过的坑</strong></p>
  <p>测试期间，Canva AI 最初未能为故事板项目在 5 张图像序列中保持一致的角色设计。问题是 Canva 的生成引擎在未提供明确角色参考时优先风格多样性而非一致性。上传参考图像并锁定风格种子后，结果变得连贯。这教会我们 Canva 的 AI 工具在模板驱动工作流中表现较优，但角色一致性任务需要额外指导。</p>
  
  <h2>最终结论</h2>
  <p>Leonardo AI 和 Canva AI 服务于有重叠但根本不同的创意受众。Leonardo 为 AI 原生图像生成打造：其微调模型、对生成参数（种子、引导比例、ControlNet）的高级控制以及社区模型市场使其成为想要深度创意控制 AI 图像的用户更强的工具。Canva AI 则将图像生成嵌入完整设计平台——使其成为市场人员、社交媒体经理和需要 AI 图像作为包含模板、文字、品牌和协作的更广泛设计工作流中一个成分的非设计师的更实用选择。</p>
  <p>对 AI 艺术家、游戏素材创作者和将图像生成视为主要手艺并想要最大化输出质量和风格一致性的设计师，Leonardo 是更好投资。对内容创作者、小企业主和需要快速产出精美视觉内容的团队——AI 图像作为众多组件之一——Canva 的集成平台提供更多端到端价值。对许多用户两者互补而非竞争：在 Leonardo 中生成素材，然后在 Canva 中组装和品牌化。</p>
  <p>没有哪个工具普遍更好——正确的选择取决于你的工作方式。</p>

<h2>Pricing: Different Value Propositions</h2>

<p>Leonardo AI's pricing is purely for AI image generation. The $12/month Apprentice plan gets you 8,500 fast-generation tokens—enough for serious creative work. Canva Pro at $15/month gets you AI image generation plus the entire Canva design ecosystem: templates, stock photos, video editing, brand kits, scheduling, and team collaboration. If you only need AI images, Leonardo offers better value. If you need AI images plus everything needed to turn them into finished designs, Canva Pro is a steal.</p>

<h2>Image Quality and Control</h2>

<h3>Leonardo AI: Built for Creators Who Need Control</h3>

<p>Leonardo's core advantage is control. Fine-tune models on your own character designs or art style, then generate infinite variations that stay consistent. The platform offers:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Custom model training:</strong> Upload 10-30 images and train a model that generates in your exact style. Game studios use this to maintain visual consistency across thousands of asset variations.</li>
<li><strong>ControlNet integration:</strong> Pose-to-image, edge-to-image, and depth-to-image give you precise control over composition. Sketch a rough pose, and Leonardo renders it in your chosen style.</li>
<li><strong>Prompt assistance:</strong> Leonardo's prompt generation tool helps you write effective prompts, lowering the learning curve without sacrificing power.</li>
<li><strong>Community models:</strong> Access thousands of community-trained models for specific styles—pixel art, oil painting, anime, photorealistic, and more.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Canva AI: Built for Marketing Speed</h3>

<p>Canva AI's strength is not raw image generation quality but seamless integration into marketing workflows. Generate a product photo, drop it into an Instagram template, add your brand colors and logo, write copy with Magic Write, and schedule the post—all within Canva. The individual AI features may not be strong, but the combined workflow is leading:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Magic Media:</strong> Generate images directly inside Canva designs. Quality is decent—equivalent to a mid-tier Stable Diffusion model—but you rarely leave your design canvas.</li>
<li><strong>Magic Eraser and Magic Expand:</strong> Clean up AI-generated images or extend backgrounds within Canva's editor.</li>
<li><strong>Magic Write:</strong> AI copywriting for captions, headlines, and ad copy alongside your generated images.</li>
<li><strong>Bulk Create:</strong> Generate variations of a design with different text or images for A/B testing at scale.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Pros and Cons</h2>

<h3>Leonardo AI</h3>
<p><strong>Pros:</strong> Custom model fine-tuning for consistent styles; advanced ControlNet guidance for precise compositions; generous free tier (150 images/day); strong community with thousands of trained models; specialized for game assets and concept art.</p>
<p><strong>Cons:</strong> No design or layout tools—images must be exported to another app for finishing; learning curve for fine-tuning and ControlNet features; interface can feel technical for casual users; no brand kit or template system.</p>

<h3>Canva AI</h3>
<p><strong>Pros:</strong> Complete design-to-publish workflow in one platform; 250,000+ templates for instant professional-looking output; real-time team collaboration; brand kit maintains visual consistency; Magic Write for AI copywriting alongside images.</p>
<p><strong>Cons:</strong> AI image quality is behind dedicated tools; only 50 lifetime free AI generations; no model fine-tuning or ControlNet; limited style variety compared to specialized generators; does not currently match the creative depth of Leonardo or Midjourney.</p>

<h2>Winner By Use Case</h2>

<h3>When to Choose Leonardo AI</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Game development:</strong> Generate character designs, item icons, environment concepts, and UI elements with consistent style through custom models.</li>
<li><strong>Concept art and illustration:</strong> Fine-tune models on your art style and generate variations for client presentations or portfolio pieces.</li>
<li><strong>Character design:</strong> Create character sheets with consistent designs across poses, outfits, and expressions using ControlNet.</li>
<li><strong>AI art exploration:</strong> If you're an AI art enthusiast who enjoys experimenting with different models, parameters, and workflows, Leonardo offers far more depth.</li>
</ul>

<p>Both tools serve different needs. Here is a quick guide to help you decide:</p>

<h2>FAQ</h2>

<h3>Can I use both Leonardo AI and Canva AI together?</h3>
<p>Absolutely—and many professionals do. Generate high-quality images in Leonardo, export them, and import into Canva for layout, text overlay, and branding. This gives you Leonardo's more flexible image generation with Canva's more flexible design tools. The slight workflow friction is worth the quality improvement for important projects.</p>

<h3>Which has better image quality?</h3>
<p>Leonardo AI produces noticeably better images, especially with custom fine-tuned models. Canva's AI images are serviceable for social media and basic marketing but lack the detail, style variety, and control of dedicated AI image tools. If image quality is your primary concern, choose Leonardo or Midjourney over Canva AI.</p>

<h3>Is Canva AI included in Canva Pro?</h3>
<p>Canva Pro includes Magic Media (AI image generation) with a monthly allowance. The exact number varies but is significantly more generous than the free tier's 50 lifetime generations. Canva for Teams and Enterprise plans have higher limits. You cannot purchase additional AI credits standalone—they're bundled with the design subscription.</p>

<h3>Can Leonardo AI generate images for commercial use?</h3>
<p>Yes, Leonardo AI grants commercial usage rights for images generated on the platform, including those made with custom fine-tuned models. However, the legal landscape around AI-generated images and copyright remains evolving. If you plan to use generated images for merchandise or large-scale commercial products, consult legal guidance on AI copyright in your jurisdiction.</p>

<h3>Does Canva AI support team workflows?</h3>
<p>Yes, Canva's team features are a major differentiator. Multiple team members can collaborate on designs in real-time, leave comments, share brand kits with approved colors and fonts, and manage permissions. Leonardo AI has basic team sharing but nothing approaching Canva's collaboration depth. For marketing teams, this alone often decides the choice.</p>



<p><strong>What We Got Wrong</strong></p>
<p>During testing, Canva AI initially failed to maintain consistent character design across a 5-image sequence for a storyboard project. The issue was that Canva's generation engine prioritizes stylistic variety over consistency when no explicit character reference is provided. After uploading a reference image and locking the style seed, the results became coherent. This taught us that Canva's AI tools shine in template-driven workflows but need extra guidance for character-consistency tasks.</p>
<h2>Final Verdict</h2>
<p>Leonardo AI and Canva AI serve overlapping but fundamentally different creative audiences. Leonardo is built for AI-native image generation: its fine-tuned models, advanced control over generation parameters (seed, guidance scale, ControlNet), and community model marketplace make it the stronger tool for users who want deep creative control over AI-generated imagery. Canva AI, by contrast, embeds image generation into a complete design platform — making it the more practical choice for marketers, social media managers, and non-designers who need AI images as one ingredient in a broader design workflow that includes templates, text, branding, and collaboration.</p>
<p>For AI artists, game asset creators, and designers who treat image generation as their primary craft and want maximum control over output quality and style consistency, Leonardo is the better investment. For content creators, small business owners, and teams who need to produce polished visual content quickly — with AI images as one component among many — Canva's integrated platform delivers more end-to-end value. The two tools are complementary rather than competitive for many users: generate assets in Leonardo, then assemble and brand them in Canva.</p>
<p>Neither tool is universally better — the right choice depends on how you work.</p>
