## How AI Can Help Your Finances

AI is an excellent tool for financial planning and education. It cannot replace a financial advisor, but it can help you make more informed decisions.

## Key Use Cases

### Budgeting and Expense Tracking
AI can categorize expenses, identify spending patterns, and suggest areas to cut back. Upload anonymized transaction data and ask for insights.

### Investment Education
AI explains complex financial concepts in plain language. Use it to understand ETFs, index funds, tax strategies, and retirement planning basics.

### Tax Preparation
AI can help organize deductions, explain tax rules, and prepare documentation summaries. It does not replace a CPA but makes tax season less stressful.

### Debt Payoff Planning
AI can model different debt repayment strategies (avalanche vs. snowball) and calculate the optimal payoff order based on your specific situation.

## Best Tools

- **ChatGPT / Claude:** General financial questions and planning
- **Notion AI:** Building personal finance dashboards
- **Google Sheets + AI:** Automated budget tracking with formulas
- **Perplexity:** Researching tax rules and financial products

## Safety Guidelines

1. Never share account numbers or passwords with AI
2. Anonymize financial data before analysis
3. Verify tax and legal advice with a professional
4. Use AI for education and planning, not final decisions
