## Core Tutorial: Using Perplexity for Research

### Step 1: Account Setup and Pro vs Free

Go to [perplexity.ai](https://perplexity.ai) and create a free account. The free tier gives you 5 Pro Searches per day and unlimited Quick Searches. Quick Search uses a faster but shallower model; Pro Search performs multi-step autonomous research — it generates multiple sub-queries, reads several pages per query, and synthesizes a comprehensive answer.

For serious research, the $20/month Pro plan is the practical entry point. It unlocks unlimited Pro Searches, file upload (ask questions about your own PDFs and documents), and the ability to switch between Claude Sonnet, GPT-4o, and Perplexity's default model as the underlying reasoning engine.

**Screenshot description:** Perplexity home page showing the search bar with "Where knowledge begins" tagline, Focus mode toggle, and Pro Search toggle below the input field.

### Step 2: Basic Search with Focus Mode

Perplexity's Focus feature lets you scope searches to specific source types. Toggle Focus before entering your query:

- **Web** — Default, searches the open internet broadly
- **Academic** — Restricts to academic papers, journals, and research databases (Semantic Scholar, arXiv, PubMed)
- **Writing** — Generates text without web search (similar to ChatGPT)
- **Video** — Searches YouTube and other video platforms for relevant content
- **Social** — Searches Reddit and other discussion platforms for community perspectives

For example, with Focus set to Academic and Pro Search enabled: `"What does the latest research say about LoRA fine-tuning efficiency compared to full fine-tuning?"` Perplexity returns a structured answer citing papers from arXiv, ACL, and NeurIPS proceedings — with direct links to each paper.

### Step 3: Pro Search for Deep Investigations

Pro Search is the core differentiator. It transforms a single question into a multi-step research process:

1. Perplexity generates 3–7 sub-queries related to your question
2. It searches the web for each sub-query independently
3. It reads the top 2–3 results per sub-query (typically 10–15 total sources)
4. It synthesizes all findings into a coherent answer with inline citations

Example: `"Compare the enterprise security features of Notion, Confluence, and Coda, including compliance certifications, access controls, and data residency options."` Pro Search queries each tool's security page, trust center, and third-party reviews, then produces a structured comparison with citations to each vendor's official documentation.

### Step 4: Collections for Organized Research

Collections are persistent research projects. Create a Collection for each ongoing topic (e.g., "Q3 Competitor Analysis"), add relevant Pro Search queries, and the AI maintains context across all entries. You can also upload files (PDFs, spreadsheets) to a Collection and ask questions directly about those documents.

This is particularly valuable for long-running investigations — instead of re-establishing context with each query, the Collection remembers what you have already researched and builds on it.

### Failure Case: The Outdated Source Problem

**What we tried:** Using Perplexity to research "the latest pricing for Midjourney subscription plans" in July 2026, expecting the current rates.

**What went wrong:** Pro Search returned Midjourney's pricing from a blog post that was last updated in November 2025 — before the v7 pricing restructure. The cited source was a reputable tech blog, but they had not updated their article. Perplexity presented the outdated pricing as current because the source appeared authoritative and recent. The error was subtle: numbers had shifted by $10–$20 across tiers, enough to mislead a purchasing decision.

**How we fixed it:** We switched to Focus: Social and searched `"Midjourney pricing 2026 Reddit"` to find recent user discussions confirming the current rates. We then verified against Midjourney's official pricing page directly. The lesson: Perplexity's answer quality is bounded by its sources. For time-sensitive queries, cross-check with official documentation or set a custom date filter (available in Pro settings).

**Lesson:** Perplexity amplifies source quality — it does not replace source verification. For critical research, use the citation links to spot-check claims, especially for pricing, dates, and rapidly changing information.

### Step 5: File Upload and Document Q&A

Pro users can upload PDFs, Word documents, and spreadsheets. After uploading, ask questions directly about the document's content. This is useful for:

- Analyzing a 50-page industry report — ask specific questions rather than reading every page
- Cross-referencing a contract against regulatory requirements
- Extracting structured data from unstructured PDFs (e.g., financial tables from an annual report)

Uploads are private to your account; Perplexity states they are not used for training.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Perplexity a search engine or a chatbot?**
A: Both. It answers like a chatbot with natural language understanding but sources every claim from the live web — combining the conversational interface of an AI assistant with the verifiability of a search engine.

**Q: Does Perplexity replace Google?**
A: For research questions, product comparisons, and anything where source verification matters, Perplexity is often faster than opening 10 Google tabs and reading each page. For navigational search (finding a specific company's login page, a known URL), Google is still the more direct tool. Many researchers use both: Perplexity for investigation, Google for navigation.

**Q: Can Perplexity access paywalled content?**
A: No. Perplexity searches publicly available web content. It cannot access articles behind paywalls (e.g., Wall Street Journal, Nature, The Economist) unless a free preview is available. For academic research, it accesses open-access databases like arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and PubMed.

**Q: How reliable are Perplexity's sources?**
A: Source quality varies. Perplexity tends to cite established publications, official documentation, and academic papers for most queries. However, it can surface lower-quality sources (medium blog posts, outdated articles) when authoritative sources are scarce on a topic. Always click through and verify critical claims.

**Q: Does Perplexity store my search history?**
A: Yes, in your account's Threads and Collections. You can delete individual threads or entire Collections. Perplexity's privacy policy states they may use anonymized queries for service improvement; Pro users have additional data controls. For sensitive research, consider the Pro plan's enhanced privacy options.

**Q: What is the difference between Pro Search and Quick Search?**
A: Quick Search provides a fast, single-pass answer using a simpler model — good for straightforward questions with clear answers. Pro Search performs multi-step autonomous research: it generates sub-queries, reads 10–15 sources, and synthesizes a comprehensive answer with detailed citations. Quick Search takes 2–5 seconds; Pro Search takes 15–60 seconds.
