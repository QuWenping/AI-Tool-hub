**Q: How does PlayHT voice cloning work?**

Requires a clean 30-second to 3-minute audio sample. AI extracts vocal characteristics (pitch, timbre, pace, breathing patterns, emotional range) and builds a mathematical model. Cloned voice generates speech in that person's voice across supported languages. Requires consent verification.
