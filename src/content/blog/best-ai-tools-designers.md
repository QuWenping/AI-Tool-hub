## How We Tested

Over 30 days, we evaluated 12 AI-powered design tools across image generation, UI design, and photo editing. Each tool was scored on output quality, ease of use, creative control, and value.

## Top Picks

### Best Overall: Midjourney
Midjourney V7 brings unprecedented photorealism and style control. Best for concept art, marketing visuals, and product renders.

### Best for UI/UX: Figma AI
Auto-rename layers, text-to-UI generation, and intelligent design suggestions make it indispensable for interface designers.

### Best for Photo Editing: Adobe Firefly
Integrated directly into Photoshop, Firefly offers generative fill and style transfer with commercial-safe outputs.

### Best Free Option: Canva AI
Magic Write, Magic Design, and Background Remover are excellent for social media and quick designs.

## The Full Ranking

1. Midjourney — Best image quality
2. Figma AI — Best for UI/UX
3. Adobe Firefly — Best for professional editing
4. Canva AI — Best ease of use
5. Stable Diffusion — Best for customization
6. Leonardo AI — Best for game assets
7. Ideogram — Best text-in-image
8. Krea AI — Best real-time generation
9. ComfyUI — Best advanced workflows
10. DALL-E 3 — Best quick illustrations

## Verdict

For most designers: Midjourney + Figma AI + Canva AI covers 90% of needs.
