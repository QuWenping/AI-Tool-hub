## AI for Teachers

### Lesson Planning
AI can generate lesson outlines, discussion questions, and activity ideas in minutes. Teachers save 3-5 hours per week on prep work.

### Personalized Learning
AI adapts explanations to different learning styles. A visual learner gets diagrams; a verbal learner gets analogies. Best tools: ChatGPT, Claude.

### Grading Assistance
AI can evaluate short-answer questions, provide initial feedback, and flag areas where students need help. It does not replace teacher judgment but accelerates the process.

## AI for Students

### Research and Writing
AI helps students find sources, outline papers, and improve drafts. Tools like Elicit and Consensus find academic papers with real citations.

### Concept Mastery
AI tutors explain difficult concepts in multiple ways until they click. Students can ask unlimited questions without judgment.

### Language Learning
AI provides conversational practice, grammar correction, and cultural context. Best tools: ChatGPT Voice Mode, DeepL.

## Ethical Guidelines

1. Always disclose AI use in academic work
2. Verify AI-generated facts with primary sources
3. Use AI as a learning accelerator, not a shortcut
4. Never input personal student data into consumer AI tools
