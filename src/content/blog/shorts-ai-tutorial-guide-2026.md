## Step-by-Step: Creating Short-Form Videos with Shorts AI

### Step 1: Input Your Source Content

Shorts AI accepts three input types:

- **URL**: Paste a blog post, product page, or news article link. The AI extracts key points and generates a script automatically. In our test, pasting a 1,500-word blog post URL produced a usable short-form script in under 30 seconds.
- **Text prompt**: Type a topic or idea directly (e.g., "3 productivity tips for remote workers"). The AI generates both a script and suggested visuals. Best for original content where you do not have an existing article to draw from.
- **Custom script**: Write or paste your own script. This bypasses the AI script writer and gives you full creative control over the narrative.

For content repurposing (blog-to-video, product-to-Reel), the URL input is the most efficient path. The AI script writer uses a hook-driven structure — it opens with a pattern interrupt, delivers value in stacked points, and closes with a call-to-action — which aligns with short-form content engagement patterns on TikTok and Reels.

### Step 2: Review and Refine the AI-Generated Script

After the AI generates a script, review it carefully. Our testing revealed a consistent pattern: the AI condenses long-form content effectively (capturing the main points), but sometimes misses nuance or context that makes the original content compelling.

Edit the script by:
- **Adding specific data points** the AI skipped (e.g., statistics, pricing, or unique insights from the original article)
- **Adjusting the hook** to match your brand voice — the AI's default hooks are functional but generic
- **Shortening or lengthening** based on your target platform (TikTok favors 15-30 seconds; YouTube Shorts can run up to 60 seconds)
- **Replacing generic calls-to-action** with platform-specific CTAs ("Follow for more" vs. "Link in bio" vs. "Subscribe")

In our blog-to-TikTok test, the AI script captured the article's three key points but missed a compelling statistic that had performed well in the original post. Adding that statistic back into the script took 30 seconds and significantly improved the video's hook.

### Step 3: Customize Visuals and Captions

Shorts AI automatically matches stock footage to each sentence in your script. The footage library covers common topics (technology, business, lifestyle, nature) but is smaller than general-purpose editors. In our testing:

- **Well-matched clips (~60%)**: The AI selected footage that directly represented the script content (e.g., "open your laptop" → shot of someone opening a laptop)
- **Thematically adjacent clips (~30%)**: The footage was in the right category but not precisely matched (e.g., "schedule your day" → generic office shot rather than a calendar close-up)
- **Poor matches (~10%)**: The footage was unrelated and needed manual replacement

You can replace any clip by browsing the library or uploading your own footage. We found that uploading 3-5 custom clips (product shots, branded b-roll, screen recordings) alongside the AI-generated footage significantly improved output quality — basically using the AI for B-roll filler and your own assets for key moments.

Captions are styled automatically with animated word-by-word highlighting in a clean, readable font. You can customize colors, fonts, and positioning, but the default styling is polished enough for direct posting on most platforms.

### Step 4: Export and Post

Select your target platform (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) and export. The Pro plan ($15/month) unlocks HD exports (1080p) and removes the watermark. Free tier exports include a prominent watermark that cannot be removed — suitable for testing but not for publishing branded content.

Export times are fast: a 30-second video with AI voiceover, captions, and stock footage rendered in approximately 45 seconds in our tests. The output is optimized for 9:16 vertical format with platform-appropriate resolution and file size.
